How this planner works: Planned purchase amount = additional shares × planned buy price. Blended average cost = (current shares × current average cost + planned purchase amount) ÷ shares after purchase. Total capital = existing invested cost + planned purchase amount. NPR values use the fixed Rs 149.21/USD rate. “Add planned buy” updates the local holding assumptions only; it does not place a Revolut order.
Reset planner clears only the two planned-buy inputs and keeps your current holdings. Reset portfolio restores the starting position of 38 shares at $83.00, clears the planned purchase and saves those defaults in this browser.
